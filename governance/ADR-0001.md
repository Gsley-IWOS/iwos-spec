# ADR-0001: Establish Architecture Decision Records

**Status:** Accepted  
**Date:** 2026-07-31  
**Decision Owner:** IWOS Architecture Authority  
**Applies To:** IWOS Specification Repository

---

## Context

IWOS is governed by durable architecture and explicit decisions. Architectural choices must remain traceable after implementation details, contributors, and priorities change.

Without a formal decision record process, architectural knowledge becomes fragmented across conversations, issues, pull requests, and implementation artifacts. This weakens institutional memory and makes it difficult to understand why the system evolved in a particular direction.

## Decision

IWOS will use Architecture Decision Records (ADRs) as the authoritative mechanism for recording significant architecture decisions.

Each ADR must document:

- the decision being made;
- the context that required the decision;
- the options considered when relevant;
- the chosen outcome;
- the consequences and tradeoffs;
- the supersession relationship if the decision is replaced.

ADRs are immutable after acceptance except for administrative corrections, clarification notes, or explicit supersession references.

## Consequences

Accepted ADRs become part of the architectural memory of IWOS. Implementation work must conform to accepted ADRs unless a later ADR supersedes them.

Architectural changes without corresponding ADR coverage are incomplete and must not be treated as authoritative.

## Governance

New ADRs must be proposed through normal repository review. An ADR becomes accepted only when approved by the designated architecture authority and merged into the repository.

Rejected or superseded ADRs must remain in the repository to preserve historical traceability.

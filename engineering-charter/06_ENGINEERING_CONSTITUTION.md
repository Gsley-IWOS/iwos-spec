# Purpose

The Engineering Constitution establishes the supreme engineering law for IWOS beneath the Founding Charter.

It defines the authority hierarchy, mandatory engineering principles and non-negotiable rules that govern all IWOS engineering activity.

---

# Authority

The Engineering Constitution is subordinate only to the Founding Charter.

All programme documents, architecture documents, playbooks, contracts, standards, specifications, schemas, templates, workflows, examples and implementations are subordinate to this constitution unless the Founding Charter states otherwise.

Where subordinate documents conflict with this constitution, this constitution prevails.

---

# Sections

## 1. Constitutional Hierarchy

IWOS engineering authority follows this order:

1. Founding Charter.
2. Engineering Constitution.
3. Project Genesis.
4. Architecture Baseline.
5. Ratified governance processes, standards and specifications.
6. Engineering playbooks and contracts.
7. Approved decisions and work packages.
8. Implementation.
9. Verification evidence and release artefacts.
10. Engineering memory.

Implementation shall never override constitutional authority.

## 2. Supreme Engineering Principles

All engineering work shall uphold:

- Mission first.
- Architecture before code.
- Knowledge as infrastructure.
- Verifiable intelligence.
- Human authority.
- Excellence over convenience.
- Continuous learning.

## 3. Architecture Supremacy

Architecture is a governing asset, not an implementation side effect.

No contributor or AI agent may redefine architecture through implementation.

Architectural evolution shall follow the formal sequence defined by the Architecture Baseline.

## 4. Human Authority

Humans define purpose, approve authority changes and remain accountable for merge, release and governance decisions.

AI agents may recommend, draft, verify and implement within authorised scope, but they do not hold final authority.

## 5. Verification Duty

Every engineering activity shall produce evidence proportionate to its risk and scope.

Verification may include document validation, reference checks, tests, static analysis, security review, performance review and maintainability review.

Completion without verification is not complete.

## 6. Knowledge Duty

Knowledge shall be preserved as a first-class engineering asset.

Engineering work shall improve institutional memory, maintain traceability and avoid losing significant decisions or rationale.

## 7. Scope and Change Control

Work shall remain within authorised scope.

Contributors shall not mix unrelated changes into a work package.

Changes to governance, standards, schemas, templates, workflows, examples and implementation repositories require explicit authorisation from the applicable milestone or governance process.

## 8. Compatibility and Integrity

IWOS engineering shall optimise for long-term correctness, clarity, maintainability, observability, reliability, extensibility, testability and performance.

Short-term convenience shall not compromise architectural integrity.

## 9. Agent Compliance

All AI agents participating in IWOS engineering shall comply with the AI Agent Contract.

Agents shall load mandatory documents, verify repository state, respect authority boundaries, avoid prohibited actions and produce reviewable evidence.

## 10. Review and Merge Law

No change may be merged without satisfying mandatory review gates and merge criteria established by the Code Review Playbook and applicable standards.

Human approval is required for merge decisions.

## 11. Ratification

Engineering Constitution EC-001 is ratified as the supreme engineering law of IWOS beneath the Founding Charter.

It remains in force until superseded by a formally ratified Engineering Constitution release.

# IWOS Engineering Constitution

**Purpose:** Define the governance authority, amendment model, and engineering obligations for IWOS specification work.

**Authority:** engineering-charter/00_FOUNDING_CHARTER.md

**Version:** 1.0.0

**Status:** Ratified bootstrap baseline

**Parent document:** engineering-charter/00_FOUNDING_CHARTER.md

---

## Purpose

The Engineering Constitution governs how `iwos-spec` evolves. It converts the Founding Charter into enforceable repository practice.

## Authority Chain

Repository authority is interpreted in this order:

1. Founding Charter.
2. Engineering Constitution.
3. Architecture Baseline.
4. Approved ADRs.
5. Ratified standards and schemas.
6. Approved templates and examples.
7. Work packages and implementation guidance.

## Contributor Obligations

Contributors must work from approved scope, preserve document authority, validate machine-readable artifacts, and provide reviewable evidence for material changes.

## Architectural Governance

Architecture may be clarified by conforming specification documents. Architecture may be changed only through approved governance, normally an ADR or AIP reviewed by human maintainers.

## AI Governance

AI agents may draft, edit, analyze, validate, and prepare pull requests. AI agents must obey the AI Agent Contract, disclose validation performed, and leave final approval and merge authority to humans.

## Amendment Model

Amendments to constitutional or architectural documents require explicit review, clear rationale, affected-file identification, and preservation of the authority chain.

## Ratification

This constitution is ratified for Sprint 0. Future amendments require human review and must be recorded through the repository governance process.

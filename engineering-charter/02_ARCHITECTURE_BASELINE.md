## Purpose

The Architecture Baseline establishes the normative architectural foundation for IWOS. It constrains future specifications and implementation work without introducing runtime code.

## Architectural Mission

IWOS architecture transforms human intent through governed reasoning, planning, verification, execution, learning, and improvement.

## Baseline Principles

1. **Intent-centered operation:** Work begins from explicit human or organizational intent.
2. **Traceable reasoning:** Material decisions must be explainable and linked to source authority.
3. **Planned execution:** Execution should follow reviewable plans when scope or risk warrants planning.
4. **Verification loop:** Outputs must be checked against acceptance criteria, policy, and evidence.
5. **Learning loop:** Validated outcomes should improve institutional knowledge without bypassing governance.
6. **Governed autonomy:** Automation level must be bounded by authority, risk, and review requirements.

## Specification Impact

Future architecture documents, standards, schemas, examples, ADRs, and AIPs must conform to this baseline. If new evidence requires a change, the change must be proposed through the governed architecture process.

## Ratification

This baseline is ratified for Sprint 0. Amendments require a governed architecture change and human review.

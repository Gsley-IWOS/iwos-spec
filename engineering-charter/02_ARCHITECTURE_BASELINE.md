# Purpose

Architecture Baseline AB-1.0 defines the immutable architectural foundation of the Intelligent Work Orchestration System (IWOS).

Every implementation, specification, SDK, runtime, compiler, extension and enterprise product shall conform to this baseline.

No implementation may redefine architecture.

Architecture evolves only through the formal governance process.

---

# Architectural Mission

Transform human intent into reliable, explainable, verifiable and continuously improving execution.

---

# Architectural Principles

## AP-001

Mission First

Every architectural decision must support the founding mission.

---

## AP-002

Architecture Before Code

Implementation inherits architecture.

Architecture never inherits implementation.

---

## AP-003

Knowledge is Infrastructure

Knowledge is treated as an executable engineering asset.

Knowledge is versioned.

Knowledge is queryable.

Knowledge is governed.

---

## AP-004

Everything is Verifiable

Every recommendation

Every workflow

Every decision

Every execution

must be explainable and auditable.

---

## AP-005

Modularity

Every subsystem must be independently replaceable.

Loose coupling.

Strong contracts.

Clear interfaces.

---

## AP-006

AI Native

Every component must assume collaboration between humans and artificial intelligence.

AI is not an extension.

AI is a first-class participant.

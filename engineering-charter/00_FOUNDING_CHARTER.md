# IWOS Founding Charter

**Purpose:** Establish the founding mission, authority, and enduring principles of the Intelligent Work Orchestration System.

**Authority:** Supreme constitutional authority for IWOS.

**Version:** 1.0.0

**Status:** Ratified bootstrap baseline

**Parent document:** None

---

## Purpose

The IWOS Founding Charter defines why IWOS exists and how all downstream architecture, governance, standards, and implementation work must be interpreted. It is the highest-authority document in the IWOS specification hierarchy.

## Mission

IWOS exists to transform human intent into reliable, explainable, verifiable, and continuously improving execution.

## Constitutional Scope

This charter governs the IWOS specification repository, the IWOS implementation ecosystem, and any official extension, standard, schema, or operational process that claims IWOS conformance.

## Founding Principles

1. **Mission first:** Every decision must advance the IWOS mission.
2. **Architecture before code:** Implementation conforms to approved architecture; implementation does not redefine architecture by accident.
3. **Knowledge as infrastructure:** Durable knowledge must be versioned, governed, and reviewable.
4. **Verification before completion:** Work is not complete until it can be examined against defined evidence.
5. **Human authority:** AI systems may assist, analyze, draft, and execute assigned work, but humans retain final governance authority.
6. **Operational continuity:** Repository changes must remain understandable and maintainable by future teams.

## Authority

This charter is the highest authority in the IWOS document system. When conflict exists between this charter and any lower-order document, this charter governs until the conflict is formally resolved.

## Ratification

This charter is ratified as the founding constitutional document for Sprint 0. Amendments require explicit human review and must preserve the IWOS mission and authority chain.

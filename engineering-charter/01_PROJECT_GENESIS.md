# Purpose

Project Genesis defines the initial engineering programme and delivery strategy for IWOS.

It translates the Founding Charter into an executable engineering programme without redefining the mission, architecture, standards, contracts or implementation.

Project Genesis establishes how early IWOS work is sequenced, validated and protected so that future specifications and implementations inherit a coherent foundation.

---

# Authority

This document is subordinate to the Founding Charter and provides programme-level direction for the Engineering Charter Pack.

Where Project Genesis conflicts with the Founding Charter, the Founding Charter prevails.

Project Genesis is a parent document for the Architecture Baseline and is interpreted together with the Engineering Constitution, Engineering Playbook, Code Review Playbook and AI Agent Contract.

# Sections

## 1. Engineering Programme

The IWOS engineering programme exists to build a Cognitive Operating System that transforms human intent into reliable, explainable, verifiable and continuously improving execution.

The programme is organised around constitutional engineering, architecture-led delivery and evidence-based validation.

Initial work shall prioritise durable foundations over implementation speed.

## 2. Delivery Strategy

Delivery shall proceed in controlled milestones:

1. Establish constitutional engineering authority.
2. Define architecture and engineering operating rules.
3. Define governance, standards, schemas and templates in later milestones.
4. Produce specifications before implementation.
5. Implement only against ratified architecture, contracts and standards.
6. Verify every deliverable against mission, architecture and engineering quality expectations.

This strategy preserves the hierarchy defined by the Founding Charter: Mission, Architecture, Standards, Contracts, Implementation, Verification, Release and Learning.

## 3. Programme Principles

The programme shall operate according to these principles:

- Mission before implementation.
- Architecture before code.
- Knowledge as infrastructure.
- Evidence before recommendation.
- Verification before completion.
- Human accountability for all authority decisions.
- Continuous improvement through engineering memory.

## 4. Scope Control

Each work package shall have a bounded objective and shall not introduce unrelated governance, standards, schemas, templates, workflows, examples or implementation work unless explicitly authorised by its milestone.

Engineering work shall not expand scope for convenience.

## 5. Delivery Units

Delivery units may include milestones, sprints, epics, stories and work packages.

Every delivery unit shall define:

- Objective.
- Scope.
- Inputs.
- Outputs.
- Validation criteria.
- Completion evidence.

## 6. Traceability

Every significant output shall be traceable to one or more parent documents.

Traceability shall include mission alignment, architectural alignment, authority source and verification evidence.

## 7. Quality Strategy

Quality is built into the programme through review, verification, documentation and controlled change.

A deliverable is complete only when its required documents exist, references resolve, metadata is consistent, authority hierarchy is respected and every required section is finished.

## 8. AI Collaboration

AI agents may assist with research, drafting, review, verification and implementation activities.

AI agents shall follow the AI Agent Contract, read mandatory onboarding documents before implementation and produce traceable outputs.

AI assistance does not transfer authority away from human reviewers and maintainers.

## 9. Engineering Memory

The programme shall preserve engineering memory through versioned documents, decision records, architecture records and state records as those assets are introduced by later milestones.

Knowledge gained through execution shall improve future work.

## 10. Ratification

Project Genesis PG-001 is ratified as the initial programme charter for IWOS engineering delivery.

It remains in force until superseded by a formally ratified Project Genesis release.

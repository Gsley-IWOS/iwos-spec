## Purpose

EWP-0001 established the constitutional foundation and created the directories `governance/` and `architecture/`. The Repository Standard requires `templates/`, `adr/` and `aip/` to hold templates and decision records, and the Founding Charter and Architecture Baseline prescribe a decision process that nothing yet operates. This package authorises the artefacts that make change governable: who decides, how a decision is proposed and recorded, and the templates that make every future document conformant by construction.

This package was drafted by an AI agent to give the build-out traceability. Its scope is a proposal until a Maintainer approves it.

## Objective

Make change governable in this repository: every class of change has a defined authority, process, record and template.

## Scope

- `governance/`: index, Roles and Authority, Decision Process, Change Control Policy, Versioning Policy, RFC register.
- `adr/`: index and the first Architecture Decision Records for decisions this package and EWP-0003 embody.
- `aip/`: index and the first Architecture Improvement Proposal, registering identifier families.
- `templates/`: index and templates for ADR, AIP, RFC, standard, specification and work package.
- `architecture/`: index, glossary and component catalogue derived from Architecture Baseline AB-1.0.
- `standards/README.md`: index of the ratified standards.
- This package. The work-package index is an EWP-0001 output and is updated here.

## Non-Scope

- Any change to `engineering-charter/` or to a file in `standards/` other than adding the index.
- Any specification of a component or engine; these require accepted proposals under milestone M2.
- Schemas, examples and validation tooling, which EWP-0003 authorises.
